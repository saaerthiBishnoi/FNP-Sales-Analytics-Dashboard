# FNP Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from Ferns N Petals (FNP), a gifting company specializing in products for occasions such as Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries.

The objective of this project was to build an interactive Excel dashboard that helps uncover insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and seasonal demand trends.

## Business Questions Answered

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. Which occasions generate the highest revenue?
3. Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
4. Which cities place the highest number of orders?
5. How does revenue vary across months?
6. Which customers contribute the highest revenue?
7. How does revenue vary by day of week?
8. What time of day generates the highest revenue?

---

## Key Insights

* Anniversary generated the highest revenue among all occasions.
* Morning orders contributed the highest revenue.
* Tuesday recorded the highest revenue among all weekdays.
* Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets were the top-performing product categories.
* Imphal recorded the highest order volume.
* Top customers contributed significantly to overall revenue.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Increase marketing campaigns during morning hours.
* Focus inventory planning on high-performing product categories.
* Launch loyalty programs for top-spending customers.
* Increase promotions during low-performing months.
* Strengthen logistics support in high-demand cities.
